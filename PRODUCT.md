# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One admin (the studio owner/coach) of Bronze Boxing Club, a small boxing studio in Lebanon. Uses the app on a phone between coaching sessions and on a desktop at the front desk. Not a tech person; wants to check things fast and get back to the gym floor. The app is single-tenant: this is their private tool, not a multi-user SaaS.

## Product Purpose

Run the studio's daily operations: trainee roster and dues, class schedule and attendance, payments and receipts, expenses and financials, gear inventory and sales, reminders. The home screen is a launcher: open the app, see what matters right now (date, next session), jump to a section in one tap. Success = the owner trusts the numbers and never feels lost.

## Brand Personality

Ringside premium. Dark, warm, confident: a high-end boxing club at night, leather and bronze, not a startup office. Three words: premium, grounded, punchy. The club's badge logo (fist, deep red rays, bronze ring, "BRONZE BOXING CLUB") is the identity anchor.

## Anti-references

- Corporate SaaS admin templates: rows of stat cards with big numbers, gradient icons, "dashboard kit" energy.
- Neon/gamer aesthetic: glowing edges, cyber gradients, oversaturated accents.
- Cluttered data dumps: charts and tables crammed onto the home screen.

## Design Principles

- **Launcher first**: the home screen orients and routes; deep data lives in the sections.
- **One glance, one truth**: surface only what changes a decision right now (date, next session); everything else is one tap away.
- **The badge carries the brand**: the club logo does identity work; the UI stays quiet around it.
- **Warm dark, never flat black**: tinted neutrals, bronze accents, the velvet purple glow; depth from light, not from borders.
- **Thumb-reach economy**: primary actions reachable one-handed on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG mandate; pragmatic AA targets. Honors prefers-reduced-motion (already wired). Focus-visible outlines in bronze. Tap targets ≥40px on mobile. Text contrast kept readable on the dark theme (muted text no darker than current --color-muted).
